Section 1 — Master's Programs (Coursework vs Research)

Coursework Master's (MEng, taught MSc, MBA) are usually paid by the student; Research/Thesis Master's may offer stipends or waivers.

What is needed: The 3-letter currency code the university charges international students in.Example: Enter USD for USA, GBP for UK, EUR for Germany/Netherlands, CAD for Canada, or AUD for Australia.Already pre-filled by country — only change if the site bills in a different currency.
What is needed: Pick the time/billing unit for the tuition numbers you enter below.Example: If fees say “$17,500 per semester”, pick Per Semester. If public German/Swedish Master's are free, pick Tuition-Free / Nominal Admin Fee Only.Options: Per Year (annual) · Per Semester (2x/yr) · Per Quarter/Term (3–4x/yr) · Per Credit · Total Program Fee · Tuition-Free/Nominal.

Section 2 — PhD / Doctoral Programs

Many US/Canadian PhDs waive tuition for TA/RAs; public EU PhDs (Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland) are often tuition-free salaried jobs.

What is needed: The sticker international PhD tuition fee (before assistantship waivers), matching your billing choice in Section 1.Example: Enter 0 for public German/Swedish/salaried EU PhDs where no tuition is charged; enter 34780 if non-resident grad tuition is $34,780/yr before waiver.Leave blank if PhD fees vary per program and no central figure is listed.
What is needed: Minimum or standard yearly living stipend or PhD researcher salary (numbers only, in local currency).Example: Enter 48000 if guaranteed PhD funding is $48,000/yr, or 21000 if UKRI stipend is £21,000/yr.Leave blank if the page states stipends vary by department or are not published centrally.
What is needed: How international PhD students are funded overall at this university.Example: Pick Fully funded is standard for US/Canadian PhDs with RA/TA packages or European salaried PhDs; pick Competitive scholarships for most UK/Australian PhDs.Options: Fully funded is standard · Competitive scholarships / supervisor grants · Mostly self-funded.
